VitalFlow Yoga — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 August 2026

VitalFlow Yoga is an Android fitness app published by Arulmurugan Ramu. This policy describes how the app handles data. The summary is simple: it doesn't collect any.

Data collection

VitalFlow Yoga does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app requires no account, contains no advertising, includes no analytics or tracking libraries, and makes no network connections of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Practice statistics — daily streak, session counts, and total minutes — are stored only on your device. They never leave it. Clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app deletes them permanently.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, or any other device capability.

Children

The app is intended for adults. Since no data is collected from any user, no data is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
